Emergency Response

Common Water Damage Emergencies We Handle

Every water damage situation is different. Here are the most common emergencies Staten Island homeowners call us for — and what we do about each one.

🚰 Burst Pipe or Broken Water Line

A burst pipe can dump hundreds of gallons of water into your home in minutes. We shut off the source, extract standing water with truck-mounted equipment, set up structural drying, and document everything for your insurance claim. Common in Staten Island during winter freezes — especially in older homes in St. George, Stapleton, and Port Richmond.

🏚️ Flooded Basement After Heavy Rain

Staten Island basements flood during heavy rain, nor'easters, and rapid snowmelt. We pump water, remove damaged materials, dry the structure, and prevent mold growth. Neighborhoods like Great Kills, Midland Beach, and South Beach are especially vulnerable to groundwater flooding and storm surge.

🔧 Appliance Leak or Water Heater Failure

Dishwashers, washing machines, and water heaters fail without warning. A slow leak behind a wall can cause hidden damage for weeks before you notice. We use thermal imaging and moisture meters to find water you can't see, then dry and restore the affected areas before mold takes hold.

⚠️ Sewage Backup or Drain Overflow

Category 3 sewage backup is a biohazard that requires full containment and OSHA-compliant cleanup. We extract contaminated water, remove affected materials, sanitize and deodorize the space, and dispose of hazardous waste safely. Common during heavy rain in older Staten Island neighborhoods with combined sewer systems.
