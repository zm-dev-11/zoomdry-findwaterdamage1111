07 / Questions

What homeowners actually ask us.

We respond to water damage emergencies across all Staten Island ZIP codes around the clock, including nights, weekends, and holidays. Call (917) 969-1378 and speak directly with our team — no call center, no dispatcher delay.

Standard homeowners insurance typically covers sudden and accidental water damage like burst pipes and appliance failures. It does not cover flooding from storms or rising water — that requires a separate flood policy. Sewer backup requires a specific endorsement. We document every loss for your insurance claim and work directly with your adjuster.

Costs depend on the water category (clean, gray, or black water), the affected square footage, and whether reconstruction is needed beyond drying. We provide a written estimate before any work begins. Many jobs are covered in full or in part by homeowners insurance.

Restoration focuses on drying, cleaning, and salvaging the structure after water intrusion. Repair goes further — replacing drywall, flooring, cabinetry, and structural framing damaged beyond salvage. Anajur handles both phases under one NYC DCWP Home Improvement Contractor license.
