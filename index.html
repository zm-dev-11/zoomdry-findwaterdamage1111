FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before calling.

We guarantee on-site arrival within 90 minutes anywhere in our service area, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. No answering services. You reach our dispatch team directly on every call.
Most homeowners insurance policies cover sudden and accidental water damage: burst pipes, appliance leaks, water heater failure. Rising groundwater and coastal storm surge typically require separate flood insurance. We handle all documentation and communicate directly with your adjuster so you do not have to.
Water extraction takes a few hours. Structural drying takes 3 to 5 days on average with commercial dehumidifiers and air movers. We monitor moisture readings daily and do not pull equipment until the structure is confirmed dry. You receive daily updates with actual readings.
Do not attempt to clean mold with bleach. Bleach only addresses surface growth and can spread spores through your HVAC system. We assess, contain, remove, and treat affected areas following IICRC S520 standards. Fast response after water damage is the best mold prevention.
For covered losses, we bill your insurance company directly. Most homeowners pay only their deductible. For non-insured work, we provide a detailed written estimate before any work begins. No surprises. No hidden charges.
